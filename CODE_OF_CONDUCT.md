# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and safe for everyone, regardless of experience level, identity, background, or perspective.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and open to technical disagreement.
- Focus criticism on ideas and changes, not people.
- Protect private information, credentials, and security-sensitive details.
- Assume good intent while asking for clarification when needed.
- Help keep discussions accessible and welcoming.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, publishing uncoordinated vulnerability details, or sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository’s [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/Gowrav-M/neuralops-platform/security/advisories/new) when a private channel is needed. Maintainers may clarify expectations, remove content, limit participation, or take other proportionate action. Retaliation against a reporter is not acceptable.
